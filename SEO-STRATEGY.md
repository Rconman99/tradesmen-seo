## Phase 1: Website Foundation

### Technical Requirements
- **Mobile-first design** (60%+ of local searches are mobile)
- **Page speed < 2 seconds** (Core Web Vitals passing)
- **SSL certificate** (HTTPS required)
- **Clean URL structure**: `/services/concrete-cutting/spokane-wa/`
- **XML sitemap** auto-generated
- **robots.txt** properly configured
- **Canonical tags** to prevent duplicate content penalties


## Phase 2: Geo-Targeted Landing Pages

### The Strategy
Create ONE unique landing page for each major city/zip code in the service area.
These are NOT cookie-cutter templates with swapped city names — Google penalizes that.

### Each City Page Must Include:
1. **Unique H1**: "[Service] in [City], [State]"
2. **200-500 words** of unique local content (landmarks, neighborhoods, local context)
3. **Service list** specific to what's available in that area
4. **Embedded Google Map** centered on that city
5. **Local phone number** (or tracking number per city)
6. **Customer testimonial** from that area (if available)
7. **Photos** of completed work in that area
8. **CTA**: "Get a Free Quote in [City]"
9. **LocalBusiness schema markup** (JSON-LD)
10. **Internal links** to related service pages

- [ ] Primary category: "Concrete Cutting Service" or "Concrete Contractor"
- [ ] Secondary categories: "Demolition Contractor", "Construction Company"
- [ ] Complete ALL fields (hours, services, attributes)
- [ ] Add 20+ high-quality photos (jobs, equipment, team, before/after)
- [ ] Write 750-word business description with keywords
- [ ] Add all service areas (cities served)
- [ ] Enable messaging
- [ ] Add products/services with pricing ranges
- [ ] Weekly Google Posts (offers, completed projects, tips)

### Review Strategy
- **Goal**: 50+ reviews with 4.8+ average
- Send review request link after EVERY completed job
- Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours
- Use keywords naturally in responses: "Thank you for choosing us for your concrete cutting project in Spokane!"
- Never buy fake reviews — Google catches and penalizes this
